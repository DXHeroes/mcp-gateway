Running the official DXH Gateway Enterprise Edition container image requires
a separately issued, signed offline license and the applicable agreement with
DX Heroes. The license identifies the customer, paid or trial type, validity
period and active-user limit. A null user limit means unlimited users. Trial
licenses are issued individually; their signed expiry defines trial duration.
Paid and trial licenses enable the same EE product features.

Seats are for people, not for applications. Every active local account
and every active verified external identity of a person consumes one seat
across the installation. External identities are distinct by verified issuer
and subject; identities are not merged by email. All roles count. Sessions and
user-owned keys do not consume additional seats.

Applications and services that call the gateway on their own behalf do not
consume seats. An external identity is treated as an application when its
verified token carries no subject and no claim that identifies a person, and
names the calling client in the configured client id claim. A token that
carries a subject cannot be told apart from a person, so its identity consumes
a seat even when it belongs to an application. Deactivating an identity denies
it access and frees its seat, if it consumes one.

Both paid and trial licenses retain full functionality for 30 days after expiry.
After that period, existing operation, login, credential renewal, exports and
security restrictions/revocations continue. Creating users, connections or
profiles, widening permissions and other configuration changes are restricted.
Renewal removes these restrictions without deleting data. The installation
does not convert to CE automatically.

agreed separately. Individual written agreements remain controlling; these
